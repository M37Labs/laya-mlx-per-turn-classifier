{# 2 · Two systems #}
The idea

Two systems, like the mind.

In Thinking, Fast and Slow, Kahneman describes a fast, intuitive System 1 that handles most decisions, and a slow, deliberate System 2 that steps in when it's needed. AI workflows can work the same way.

{# 3 · What System 1 does well #}
Why an encoder

One pass. Every answer. With confidence.

Laya-MLX and similar encoder classifiers read text rather than write it. That's what makes them fast, cheap and predictable.

01

Structured, not generated

Returns labels, scores and yes/no answers your systems can act on directly. There's no text to parse and no invented formats.

02

Many questions at once

Routing, urgency, risk and intent come from the same pass. An extra question adds milliseconds, not another model call.

03

Knows when it's unsure

Every answer carries a confidence. When it falls below your threshold, the case escalates to System 2 instead of guessing.

04

Set up in plain English

A new workflow is a list of questions and options. There's no labelled dataset to build and no retraining.
